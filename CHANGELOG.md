# Changelog

## [0.1.0] — 2026-11-04
First public release.
- Daily digest from your RSS feeds (articles and podcasts), picked by an LLM to match your interests.
- Simplify: B1-level retelling of any material.
- Words: 10–15 useful words and expressions with a ready-to-paste block for Quizlet import.
- Voice discussion of a material with corrections after every reply (Groq Whisper + LLM, edge-tts voice replies).
- Weekly review of your typical mistakes with exercises.
- Free-tier friendly: Groq with optional Gemini fallback, SQLite storage.
- Security: owner-only access, SSRF protection for feed links, prompt-injection hardening, sandboxed systemd unit and Docker setup.
- UI in English and Russian; translation language is configurable.
